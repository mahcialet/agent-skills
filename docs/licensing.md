# Licensing

Original repository material is licensed under the root MIT License. A skill
that adapts or quotes another work must include `NOTICE.md` with its source,
copyright holder, license, the revision reviewed, inherited elements, and
material modifications.

The root `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` indexes those notices. Do not copy standards
or guidance whose terms do not permit redistribution. Prefer attribution,
summary, and independently written operational rules over bulk quotation.

No skill may claim certification, official conformance, or endorsement unless
the repository has authoritative evidence for that claim.
