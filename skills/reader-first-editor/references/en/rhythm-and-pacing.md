# English rhythm and pacing

文長や構文のvariation自体を目的にしない。claim、explanation、evidence、example、caution、
actionの違いが文章のemphasisとpaceに表れ、読者が重要度を見分けられるかを確認する。

## 症状

- Many sentences begin with `This + verb`, but the pronoun has no stable referent.
- Every paragraph repeats the same topic / three bullets / summary template;
  check whether the template hides differences in purpose.
- Explicit transitions (`Additionally`, `Furthermore`, `Moreover`) appear even
  when adjacent sentences already make the relation clear.
- Main claims and qualifications receive identical sentence weight; check
  whether readers can tell which to prioritize.
- Uniform short sentences leave cause, condition, or contrast for readers to
  reconstruct.
- Long periodic sentences delay the main clause and modality, so readers must
  wait to learn the action or its strength.

## 調整

- Keep a repeated opening when it maintains a useful topic chain.
- Name the antecedent when `This` could refer to a fact, process, or the whole
  preceding paragraph.
- Remove a transition only when adjacency or syntax still expresses the same
  relation.
- Combine closely related short sentences when the result makes their
  relationship explicit without stacking conditions.
- Split at a rhetorical boundary, not at an arbitrary word count.
- Give a decision or warning proportionate prominence; subordinate examples and
  side notes without deleting them.

反復数やsentence-length varianceはtripwireであり、品質スコアではない。roleとimportanceが
異なるのに同じ型で提示されていると本文から確認できる場合だけ修正する。
