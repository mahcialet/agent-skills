# English rhythm and pacing

文長や構文のvariationは目的ではない。claim、explanation、evidence、example、
caution、actionの違いが文章のemphasisとpaceに反映されているかを見る。

## 症状

- Many sentences begin with `This + verb` without a stable referent.
- Every paragraph repeats the same topic / three bullets / summary template.
- Explicit transitions (`Additionally`, `Furthermore`, `Moreover`) appear even
  when the relation is local or obvious.
- Main claims and qualifications receive identical sentence weight.
- Uniform short sentences make cause, condition, or contrast implicit.
- Long periodic sentences delay the main clause and modality.

## 調整

- Keep a repeated opening when it maintains a useful topic chain.
- Name an antecedent when `This` could refer to a fact, process, or whole
  preceding paragraph.
- Remove a transition only when adjacency or syntax still expresses the same
  relation.
- Combine closely related short sentences when doing so makes the relation
  explicit without stacking conditions.
- Split at a rhetorical boundary, not at an arbitrary word count.
- Give a decision or warning proportionate prominence; subordinate examples and
  side notes without deleting them.

反復数やsentence-length varianceはtripwireであり、品質スコアではない。roleと
importanceが異なるのに同じ型で提示されている証拠がある場合だけ修正する。
